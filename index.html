Services

Everything from a routine clean to a full smile rebuild

Every treatment plan starts with a conversation and an honest quote — no surprise charges once you're in the chair.

Check-ups & cleaning

Routine exams, scaling, and polishing to catch issues before they become expensive ones.

Fillings & root canal

Tooth-coloured fillings and gentle root canal therapy to save teeth rather than pull them.

Braces & aligners

Traditional braces and clear aligner options for teens and adults, with flexible payment plans.

Extractions & wisdom teeth

Straightforward and surgical extractions handled with sedation options for anxious patients.

Implants & crowns

Permanent replacements for missing or badly damaged teeth, built to match your bite.

Teeth whitening

In-clinic and take-home whitening kits, done safely without damaging your enamel.

Why patients choose us

Dentistry without the dread

Most people put off the dentist because the last visit was rushed, confusing, or cost more than expected. We built Millenia around fixing that: you'll see your X-rays on screen, hear your options in plain language, and get a written quote before any treatment starts.

Dr. Wanjiru and the team have treated Utawala families since 2011, from first baby teeth to full dentures — often three generations of the same household.
